The meaning behind the name

The golden way is a way of building.

Aura means gold, brilliance and excellence. Via means the way or path. Together, AUREVIA names an approach that brings beauty and intellect into the same conversation.

It is the architecture that holds the work: a framework where business, creativity, design, photography and systems can coexist. Each brand has room to grow as part of one connected ecosystem, built thoughtfully, beautifully and sustainably.
